About Me

I am a Mathematics undergraduate interested in how mathematics, data, and finance can be used to understand and solve real-world problems.

Mathematics is my foundation. I also tutor mathematics, which has helped me learn how to explain ideas simply and help others understand them.

I am currently developing my skills in data analytics, particularly with Python, Excel, and Power BI. I am learning how to work with data, find useful patterns, and turn information into clear insights.

My long-term goal is to become a quantitative financial analyst. Along the way, I am also working to become a clear thinker, good writer, good communicator, and effective speaker.

This website is where I document that journey— what I learn, what I build, what I write, and how I think.
